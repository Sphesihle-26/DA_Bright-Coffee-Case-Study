# DA_Bright-Coffee-Case-Study
A Dataset titled “Bright coffee shop sales”, which captures daily transactional information for a coffee shop.
### Problem Statement
Bright Coffee Shop is operating without a clear understanding of how its products and sales patterns contribute to overall revenue. There is a need to analyze historical transaction data to identify key performance drivers and support strategic decision-making for business growth.

### Aim
The aim of this project is to gain and provide insights from the historical data and prepare a presentation for the CEO to assist in decision-making.

### OBJECTIVE
**Objective**
Apply analytical techniques, SQL querying, and data visualization to help Bright Coffee Shop gain insights into:
• The top-performing products by revenue
• Peak business hours throughout the day
• Sales trends across various products and time frames
• Strategic recommendations to improve sales outcomes
### Summary
A well-performing and scalable business, with the next phase of growth focused on maximizing efficiency, increasing customer spend, and unlocking untapped revenue opportunities.
